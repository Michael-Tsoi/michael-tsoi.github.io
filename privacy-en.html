Privacy Policy

Last updated: 03 November 2026

1. Data Collection

HomeItemMemo is an offline item recording application. This app does NOT collect or upload any user data to remote servers.

  • Item descriptions and photos you take are stored locally on your device via Local Storage only.
  • This app does not collect: location, phone number, contacts, other photos from your gallery, microphone access or other personal information.
  • Camera access is only for taking photos of your items. Photos stay on your device and are never uploaded automatically.

2. Data Storage

All records and images are saved locally in your browser storage. Your data will be erased if you clear browser data or uninstall the PWA. Backup files must be exported manually by you and kept safely by yourself.

3. Data Sharing

We will never sell, share or transfer any of your data to third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy. The “last updated” date at the top will be revised accordingly.
